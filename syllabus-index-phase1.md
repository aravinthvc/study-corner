# India K-12 Syllabus Index — Phase 1

**Scope of this document:** real chapter/topic lists only — no concept notes,
practice questions, or videos. This is Phase 1 of the framework (chapter
skeleton), sourced from official curricula and publicly documented textbook
contents as of September 2026. Every board below is labeled by how solid its
data is — treat that label as load-bearing, not decoration.

## 2. ICSE / ISC (CISCE) — structure only, no chapters yet

- Runs Classes 1–10 as **ICSE**, Classes 11–12 as **ISC**.
- Class 1 subjects (confirmed): Mathematics, English, Environmental Studies,
  Computer Studies, plus arts/co-curricular.
- **Class 10 (ICSE)** is organized into three subject groups — this structure
  is confirmed and matters for how a catalog would need to model electives:
  - **Group I (compulsory):** English, a second language, History/Civics/Geography
  - **Group II (choose 2–3):** Mathematics, Science (Physics/Chemistry/Biology),
    Economics, Commercial Studies, Environmental Science, additional languages
  - **Group III (choose 1, skill-based):** Computer Applications and similar
- **Class 11–12 (ISC)** subjects confirmed to include: Physics, Chemistry,
  Biology, Mathematics, English, Commerce, Business Studies — organized by
  stream, similar in spirit to CBSE's senior-secondary streaming.
- **No chapter-level data gathered yet** for any ICSE/ISC subject or class.
  ICSE textbooks aren't centrally published the way NCERT is (schools choose
  from board-approved publishers), so this will need either a specific
  publisher/edition per subject (the same requirement as Kayalini's Collins
  Maths) or CISCE's own official syllabus PDFs per subject.

## 3. IGCSE (Cambridge) — structure only

Cambridge does not organize its curriculum as fixed "chapters" the way NCERT
does — it publishes **syllabus documents** per subject, structured as content
strands/learning objectives that a textbook publisher then arranges into
chapters however they choose. This means an IGCSE "chapter list" is really a
*textbook's* chapter list (publisher-specific, like Kayalini's Collins book),
not a single official one — worth deciding early which publisher's book to
target, same as we did for CBSE Maths.

Programme stages, mapped to Indian grade-equivalents:

| Cambridge stage | Approx. age | Indian grade equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| Cambridge Primary | 5–11 | Classes 1–5 |
| Cambridge Lower Secondary | 11–14 | Classes 6–8 |
| Cambridge Upper Secondary (**IGCSE**) | 14–16 | Classes 9–10 |
| Cambridge Advanced (**A Level**) | 16–19 | Classes 11–12 |

No chapter-level or even syllabus-strand-level data gathered yet.

## 4. IB (International Baccalaureate) — structure only

Like Cambridge, IB publishes **subject guides** (content + assessment
objectives), not fixed chapters — chapter structure is set by whichever
textbook a school adopts.

| IB programme | Approx. age | Indian grade equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| **PYP** (Primary Years Programme) | 3–12 | Classes 1–5 (roughly) |
| **MYP** (Middle Years Programme) | 11–16 | Classes 6–10 |
| **DP** (Diploma Programme) | 16–19 | Classes 11–12 |

No chapter-level or subject-guide-level data gathered yet.

## Scale reminder

This document covers 4 of India's 66 school boards, and within those 4,
real chapter-level detail exists for exactly one subject (CBSE Mathematics),
not yet complete even there. The other 62 boards (54 regular + 12 open
state-level boards, per the Ministry of Education's July 2026 count) aren't
touched at all. That's the honest scale of "all boards, all grades" — this
document is a real first slice of it, not the whole thing.
